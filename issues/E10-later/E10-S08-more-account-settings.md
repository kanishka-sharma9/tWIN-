# E10-S08 More account settings

- **Epic:** E10 Later: hosting and apps
- **Status:** Backlog
- **Depends on:** E10-S03
- **Decisions:** D-030, D-042
- **Flows:** None

## Goal

A user can manage their account beyond the password.

## Scope

- Connect or disconnect Google sign-in.
- Change email, confirmed by an emailed link.
- Delete the account with its vault and files.

## Acceptance criteria

- A password account can add Google and then sign in either way.
- Deleting an account removes every file and frees the email.
